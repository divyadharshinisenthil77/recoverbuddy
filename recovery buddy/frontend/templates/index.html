⏱️ 3-Minute Quick Demo Guide for Evaluators

  1. Observe Baseline vs. RecoverBuddy: Use the top-right toggle switch to see how Baseline returns cold static text ("Data received") while RecoverBuddy generates plain-language progress explanations and streaks.
  2. Test Safety Guardrails: Trigger Scenario C. Notice that the patient never receives an autonomous diagnosis like "You have an infection"; instead, an auditable High-Priority task is dispatched to Nurse Sarah.
  3. Review Care Team Accountability: Switch to the Care Team Dashboard tab to inspect the real-time escalation queue, owner assignment, and 1-click task resolution.
  4. Inspect Quantitative Evidence: Switch to the Experiment Analytics tab to view the reproducible 8-week longitudinal trial results and error diagnostics.
